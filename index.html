About Vitreo

Founded in 2000, Vitreo Homeware Ltd. grew from a local artisan workshop into a regional premium glassware brand. We make custom glassware for everyday use and special occasions, combining the care of our workshop beginnings with digital design tools and precision manufacturing.

Our range includes drinking glasses, wine glasses, tea cups, coffee mugs, bowls, jars and decorative collections. We also make personalized gifts, corporate branded glassware and limited edition artisan pieces. We work with homeowners, gift buyers, hospitality businesses and corporate clients who want glassware suited to a particular setting, occasion or brand.

Custom projects begin with a digital design consultation. We use computer aided design and digital renderings to show how a piece will look before production. Precision glass forming gives the design its shape, and laser engraving adds personalized details where appropriate. Finished pieces go through quality checks before packaging.

Recycled materials and recyclable packaging are part of our approach to manufacturing. Vitreo uses recycled glass in production and aims to achieve carbon neutral production by 2035. We also support community engagement as part of our sustainability work beyond the walls of our workshop.

Customers can find Vitreo products online and through retail partners, hospitality channels and export distributors. We plan to broaden our premium product range and expand into Europe, North America and the Middle East. As we grow, our focus remains on crafting glassware that feels at home on an everyday table and can be personalized for a gift, a hospitality setting or a corporate collection.
